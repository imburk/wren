SOUND & CONTENT

SOUNDTRACK

Every area has its own band-style theme with chanted "whoa-oh" vocals. For real sung lyrics, each theme below comes with original English lyrics and a style description. Make a sung version with any music app, then load the audio file into its slot. Loaded songs stay saved in this browser.

DUNGEON FILES

Each dungeon is its own .json file. Load one to play it straight away. It's a quick way to test dungeons you design. "Copy template" gives you a small working example to start from.
